# Superstore Sales Metrics Dashboard (2019)

## 📊 Project Overview

This analysis explores the Superstore sales dataset to uncover patterns in customer behavior, product performance, regional sales trends, and shipping logistics. By analyzing this data, the goal is to generate actionable insights that drive better decision-making in sales strategy, inventory control, customer segmentation, and delivery optimization.

---

## 🗂️ Data Source

The dataset used for this project is the globally recognized **Superstore Sales Dataset**, which contains order records, product information, shipping details, and customer demographics commonly used in business intelligence and data visualization training.

---

## 🛠️ Tools and Methodology

- **Microsoft Excel**: For initial data exploration and cleaning and Dashboard creation

---

## 📈 Dashboard Overview

### Sales Movement Analysis
- Total Revenue: **$2,261,536.78**
- Peak Month: **November**  
- Star Product: **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier**
- Sales peaked in November and September, with the lowest in February.

### Customer and Segment Analysis
- Premium Customer: **Sean Miller** ($25,043.05 in purchases)
- Leading Segment: **Consumer** ($1,148,060.53)
- High-value customers contribute disproportionately to revenue.

### Product & Category Insights
- Leading Categories:  
  - **Technology**: $827,455.87  
  - **Office Supplies**: $728,658.58  
  - **Furniture**: $705,422.33  
- Canon copier outperformed all other products by sales value.

### Region & Shipping Overview
- Top Region: **West** ($710,219.68)
- Top State: **California** ($446,306.46)
- Standard Class is the dominant delivery mode (59%)
- FedEx and UPS are major contributors to delivery efficiency.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🧍 Customer Behavior
- The **Consumer** segment generates over 50% of total revenue.
- Top 5 customers contribute to a significant share of profits.

### 🌎 Regional Trends
- The **West region** leads in total sales, followed by the **East**.
- Sales in the **South** lag behind, offering room for market growth.

### 🚚 Shipping Strategy
- **Standard Class** is most used, offering a cost-efficient delivery method.
- Most expensive products tend to be delivered via First or Second Class.

### 🛒 Product Performance
- Technology is the top-selling category.
- Items like **Canon imageCLASS Copiers** and **Binding Machines** are high-value drivers.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Focus marketing efforts on the Consumer segment**, particularly in California and New York.
2. **Promote top-performing products** with upselling and bundling strategies.
3. **Maintain optimal inventory** of technology and office supply items to prevent stockouts.
4. **Standardize delivery incentives** around Standard Class for cost savings.
5. **Explore regional growth** opportunities in the South by analyzing local market needs.

---

## 📌 Conclusion

This Superstore Sales Metrics analysis provides a 360° view of business operations from customer loyalty to shipping logistics. It empowers stakeholders to make informed decisions across marketing, sales, inventory, and delivery functions. With the right strategic implementations, the company can drive growth, improve customer experience, and enhance profitability.
